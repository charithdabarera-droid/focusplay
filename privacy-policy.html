FocusPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

FocusPlay is made by Jude Dabarera, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("I", "me"). This policy explains how FocusPlay handles your information. The short version: FocusPlay does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. No data collection

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting services.
  • FocusPlay does not send your data to me or to any server. The app works fully offline after it is installed, except the optional Walle page (see section 3A).

2. Data stored on your device

Your tasks, streaks, routines, reminders, nudges, water count, theme choice, language and suggestions are saved only in your device's local app storage. I cannot see or access it. You can export a backup file or delete everything at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

3. Notifications

If you allow it, FocusPlay schedules local notifications on your device for reminders and gentle nudges. They are created and delivered by your device's operating system. No push server is used and nothing is sent over the internet.

3A. Daily Learn (needs internet)

The Daily Learn page (word, phrase and quote of the day) only works when your device is online. It downloads read-only learning content files (word lists, phrases and quotes) from the same website that hosts the app. No personal data is sent: no account, no identifiers and no analytics. As with any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may see standard technical data such as your IP address, under its own privacy policy. Daily Learn reminders are local notifications, as in section 3.

3B. Microphone (Walle Kids only)

Walle Kids lets a child talk and hear Walle, a friendly robot, repeat it in a funny voice. FocusPlay asks for microphone permission only when Kids mode starts listening, after showing an explanation. The recording is processed in your device's memory, played back once and then discarded. Audio is never saved, never uploaded and never sent anywhere. Kids mode works offline. You can deny or revoke microphone permission at any time in your device settings; the rest of the app keeps working.

3C. Parent PIN

The Kids mode parent PIN is stored only on your device as a salted, one-way hash (PBKDF2-SHA-256). The PIN itself is never stored or transmitted.

4. Suggestion box

When you tap "Send" in the suggestion box, FocusPlay opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to charithdabarera@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself. If you do, I receive only what you put in the email (and your email address), and I use it only to read and reply to your suggestion. I do not share it.

5. Purchases

FocusPlay is a one-time paid app. Payment is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play, Apple App Store or Huawei AppGallery) under their own privacy policies. I do not receive your payment details.

6. Children

FocusPlay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Kids mode uses the microphone on the device only, as described in section 3B, and has no chat, links, purchases or ads.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

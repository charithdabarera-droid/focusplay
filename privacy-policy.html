FocusPlay Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

FocusPlay is made by Jude Dabarera, Dubai, United Arab Emirates ("I", "me"). This policy explains how FocusPlay handles your information. The short version: FocusPlay does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. No data collection

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, no tracking, no analytics, no crash reporting services.
  • FocusPlay does not send your data to me or to any server. The app works fully offline after it is installed.

2. Data stored on your device

Your tasks, streaks, routines, reminders, nudges, water count, theme choice, language, suggestions and referral code are saved only in your device's local app storage. I cannot see or access it. You can export a backup file or delete everything at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

3. Notifications

If you allow it, FocusPlay schedules local notifications on your device for reminders and gentle nudges. They are created and delivered by your device's operating system. No push server is used and nothing is sent over the internet.

4. Suggestion box

When you tap "Send" in the suggestion box, FocusPlay opens your own email app with a pre-filled message to charithdabarera@gmail.com. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email yourself. If you do, I receive only what you put in the email (and your email address), and I use it only to read and reply to your suggestion. I do not share it.

5. Refer-a-friend codes

Referral codes are generated randomly on your device. Sharing a code uses your device's normal share sheet; FocusPlay does not track who uses codes.

6. Purchases

FocusPlay is a one-time paid app. Payment is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play, Apple App Store or Huawei AppGallery) under their own privacy policies. I do not receive your payment details.

7. Children

FocusPlay does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

9. Contact
